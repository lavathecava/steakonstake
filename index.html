Our story

We put our reputation on the line — every plate.

Steak on Stake started with a simple belief: great beef deserves respect, real fire, and no shortcuts. We partner with ranchers who share our standards, then let the grill do the talking.

  • Locally sourced whenever possible
  • Open-kitchen flame grills in every location
  • House-butchered cuts, cut to order
